draw; it is a relative cost signal, not a bill (the dollar cost model is M3).

## Analyze

What the numbers show:

- **fp16 is free here**: same predictions as fp32 on all 872 sentences, half the weights, about 3
  to 3.5 times the throughput and a quarter of the energy per request. It is the better reference
  for the quantized variants.
- **int8 costs almost nothing in accuracy**: 1 flipped prediction with plain round-to-nearest, 4
  with LLM.int8. Weights drop from 127.7 MiB (fp16) to 87 MiB; the remaining 46 MiB are the
  embeddings and head.
- **int4 has a measurable cost**: RTN int4 changed 14 predictions and lost 0.92 points. NF4
  changed even more predictions (18) but lost only 0.23 points, because its flips went both ways
  (some wrong answers became right). Flips, not only accuracy, show how far a variant drifts.
- **Smaller is not faster without kernels**: the RTN variants dequantize every weight in every
  call, so they are slower than fp16; LLM.int8 is the slowest of all on this small model (its
  mixed int8 and float16 decomposition adds work that a model this size does not win back; not profiled). NF4 is the fastest quantized variant and close to fp16. On this GPU,
  the memory saved by int8 and int4 matters more than speed: all variants fit easily in 6 GB.
- **Single-request latency is dominated by overhead**: fp32 and fp16 both take about 4 to 4.4 ms per
  request; tokenization and kernel launches, not arithmetic, set the floor at batch 1.

Defects found and their root causes:

| # | Defect | Root cause | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The same variant's throughput differed by up to 4.6 times between runs (fp16: 443 then 2,034 sentences/s; fp32 measured first: 79, measured last in the second run: 491) | The laptop GPU starts in a low-clock state and ramps up under load, and other work shares the machine; the first variant measured paid for the ramp, and a single throughput round could not show it | [before-warmup-1.json](runs/before-warmup-1.json), [before-warmup-2.json](runs/before-warmup-2.json) |
| 2 | Placeholder hashes for two small model files in the first version of `sources.py` | Written before the files were fetched | caught before any run by `test_pins_are_complete` (now requires non-zero hashes) and by hashing the real files |
| 3 | `mypy --strict` rejected the bitsandbytes and footprint calls | Untyped third-party constructor; `hasattr` narrowing to `Tensor` | first type check |
